For private equity sponsors and operators of specialty pharmacies

Therapies, payer contracts, referrals and dispensing volume all rise. Access, service capacity, speed to therapy, margin and cash do not move with them.

The constraint is rarely one workflow or one technology. It is the interaction among referral intake, benefits investigation, prior authorization, financial assistance, dispensing and adherence support.

What grows Therapies · payer contracts · referral sources · patients · dispensing volume · locations
What should follow Faster access decisions · shorter time to therapy · better adherence · reliable delivery · collected cash

The answer is not automatically more technology
The right intervention may be simplification, standardization, a shared capability, better system linkage, conventional automation or selective AI. The objective is the simplest intervention capable of changing the economics.
