Bring the messy question

Early healthtech companies run into complicated decisions fast.

A promising pilot has no obvious path to revenue.

A customer wants something that could take the product in a completely different direction.

You're not sure whether you need more evidence, a different buyer, a different price, or just more time.

A design partner could accelerate the company or quietly turn you into a custom development shop.

And generic startup advice only gets you so far when healthcare brings buyers, users, regulators, reimbursement, clinical workflows, and enterprise requirements into the same decision.

This group is a place to work through those questions before they become expensive.

There is no fixed curriculum.

You bring the real decision. We pressure-test it together.

The goal is not to leave with more ideas.

It is to leave with a clearer decision and a better next move.

You might bring
  • —Should we take this pilot?
  • —Who is actually the buyer?
  • —What should we charge?
  • —Is this customer request signal or noise?
  • —What evidence do we need before selling?
  • —Are we building too much?
  • —Do we need to think about FDA yet?
  • —What needs to be true before we raise?
  • —Should we hire for this or keep it founder-led?
  • —Which market or use case should we pursue first?

How it works

6 founders

A small, curated cohort of founders at roughly similar stages. We'll avoid obvious competitive conflicts.

Six months

Enough time to see decisions unfold, learn from one another, and bring the next problem back to the group.

One 90-minute session each month

Sessions focus on the real issues members are facing, not hypothetical case studies or lectures.

Private group channel

A place to ask quick questions, share resources, and continue conversations between sessions.

$1,500 for the full six months

The founding cohort rate works out to $250/month.

Who it's for

This group is designed for founders building in digital health, healthcare software, AI, diagnostics, medical devices, clinical infrastructure, care delivery technology, and adjacent healthcare businesses.

You'll get the most from it if

You're pre-seed or early seed, including founders preparing for their first pre-seed raise.

You're past the idea stage, but may still be pre-revenue.

You're starting to have real conversations with customers, partners, investors, or regulators.

You don't yet have a full executive team around you.

And you're willing to bring the question you haven't figured out yet.
