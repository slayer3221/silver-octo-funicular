The technology is rarely the whole problem.

Value gets trapped in the decisions around it.

The proof doesn't answer the decision.

The product performs, but the evidence does not tell a buyer, executive or investor what to do.

The workflow absorbs the gain.

Technology saves time in one step while review, handoffs or downstream work consume the benefit somewhere else.

Interest never becomes commitment.

There is a champion, pilot or internal sponsor, but no economic owner willing and able to act.

Deployment doesn't become adoption.

The system goes live without becoming the normal way work gets done.

Scale creates complexity instead of leverage.

Each customer, site, market or workflow adds another exception.

No one owns the consequential decision.

Product, clinical, regulatory, security, finance and operations can each make rational local decisions while the overall economics fail.
