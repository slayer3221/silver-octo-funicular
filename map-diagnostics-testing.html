For private equity sponsors and operators of diagnostics and distributed testing businesses

Tests, channels, collection sites and volume all rise. Access, processing capacity, turnaround time, margin and cash do not move with them.

The constraint is rarely one workflow or one technology. It is the interaction among test selection, ordering, patient activation, collection, logistics, processing, result delivery and payer requirements.

What grows Tests · ordering channels · collection sites · devices · patient volume · partners
What should follow Completed tests · reliable samples · higher throughput · shorter turnaround · clean claims · collected cash

The answer is not automatically more technology
The right intervention may be simplification, standardization, a shared capability, better system linkage, conventional automation or selective AI. The objective is the simplest intervention capable of changing the economics.
